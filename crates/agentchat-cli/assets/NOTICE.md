# Banner notice

The committed ASCII banner is generated by the fixed CraftGuild Standards engine from the Small Slant FIGlet font by Glenn Chappell (June 1993), with Paul Burton’s December 1996 layout update. The original permission and distribution terms are retained in [FIGlet-LICENSE](FIGlet-LICENSE). The unchanged font source is [smslant.flf at FIGlet commit 202a0a8110650a943f1125f536b3bb455cf72ee1](https://github.com/cmatsuoka/figlet/blob/202a0a8110650a943f1125f536b3bb455cf72ee1/fonts/smslant.flf). The font and renderer are build-tool inputs, not runtime libraries of agentchat.
